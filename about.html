About Kaaj Tools

Simple tools for Indian small businesses

"Kaaj" means work. Kaaj Tools builds simple web tools that help shopkeepers, freelancers and small businesses do everyday work faster: making GST bills, tracking recurring expenses, and more tools to come.

Our tools open in your browser, need no sign-up, and keep your data on your own device. When a tool has a paid Pro version, you pay once. There are no monthly fees.
