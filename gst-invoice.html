What you get

Proper GST bills

  • HSN / SAC code for each item
  • CGST + SGST, or IGST for out-of-state sales
  • Your GSTIN and your customer's GSTIN
  • Amount in words (Indian system)
  • Authorized signatory box

Made for the counter

  • Built-in list of common items and services
  • Invoice numbers go up automatically
  • Recent bills and today's sales total
  • Your shop name and logo saved on your device

Get paid faster

  • UPI QR code on every bill, or upload your own QR
  • Send the bill to the customer on WhatsApp
  • Print on A4, 3-inch or 2-inch receipt paper
  • Save as PDF
