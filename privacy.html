Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Kaaj Tools is operated by Parveen Sultana, Kolkata, West Bengal, India. This page explains what information our tools and website use.

1. Data that stays on your device

Bills, invoices, shop details, logos, customer names and other information you type into our tools are saved only in your own browser or device. We do not receive, see or store them on our servers.

2. Information we collect when you buy Pro

When you buy the Pro license, we receive from Razorpay your email address, the Payment ID, the order details and the payment status. We keep these so we can unlock Pro, restore your license on a new device and help you if there is a problem. We also keep a random device code for each device where Pro is activated, to enforce the 3-device limit. We do not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details. Razorpay handles those under its own privacy policy.

3. Services we rely on

  • Razorpay for payments.
  • Cloudflare to run our payment and license service and store license records.
  • GitHub Pages to host our website and tools.
  • Google Fonts and other content delivery services, which may receive your IP address when your browser loads fonts and scripts.
  • A QR code image service (api.qrserver.com) that draws the automatic UPI QR code in the GST Invoice Maker. It receives the UPI payment link (your UPI ID and the bill amount) to draw the QR. If you prefer not to use this service, upload your own QR image in the app.

4. Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies. The tools use your browser's local storage to remember your data and settings on your device.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the services listed above to run the tools, or if the law requires.

6. Your choices

You can delete your saved data at any time by clearing your browser's site data. To ask us to delete your license record or to ask any privacy question, email dorpon365@gmail.com. If we delete a license record, Pro can no longer be restored on new devices.

7. Children

Our tools are meant for business and general use by adults.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
